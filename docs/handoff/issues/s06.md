## Goal
Build the pre game screen in the Toy style, exactly as the UI track drew it: the black intro of about 3 s that names the own role and its goal, then the round fading in.

## Build
The scene tree, node for node, is in prime-game-ui [`docs/handoff/s06-pre-game.md`](https://github.com/xperiaroco2/prime-game-ui/blob/ui-0.2.0/docs/handoff/s06-pre-game.md) at the tag `ui-0.2.0`. The states are drawn on the review page https://claude.ai/artifact/SCREENS_URL (screen s6; uk and en, default and large text).

- On the black-screen layer (CanvasLayer 6): `Night` (ToyBackdropNight, opaque) and `V`, a centred 1152 px ToyColumnThirtyTwo: `pregame.your_role`, the role on a ToyTitlePlate (a Label raised on ToyBaseTitle), the goal and, for a dissident, the team line.
- `Hud` in the `after` state is the round HUD (ISSUE_S7), drawn only to show what the fading Night uncovers.
- States: `engineer`, `dissident`, `after`.

Rules:
- Theme type variations from the `ui-0.2.0` pack only (#288). No `add_theme_*_override` and no `theme_override_*` (`theme_test.gd`): gaps come from the spacing variations (`ToyColumn…`).
- The title plate through `ToyRaised` (placement, size and visibility on the wrapper, #289).
- Every player-facing text is a deck key through `tr()`. A key with placeholders is set from code with `auto_translate_mode = DISABLED` and rebuilt on `NOTIFICATION_TRANSLATION_CHANGED` (#208). Teammates' names are data texts with `auto_translate_mode = DISABLED`.
- Sizes and offsets are px at the 1920×1080 base (#287).

## Behaviour
- It shows in the silent pre game (#213) when the own role arrives, for about 3 s, then the round starts.
- Role: `role.engineer` or `role.dissident` on the title plate; the goal: `role.goal.engineer` or `role.goal.dissident`.
- A dissident also sees `pregame.teammate` with `{names}`: the teammates' names joined with ", ". Hidden for an Engineer and for a dissident with no teammates.
- It reads only the own role and `Teammates` (#175): nothing about other players' roles.
- One sound per role plays (#213). No input, no mic and no other HUD while it shows. Nobody hears anybody and no text says so.
- `after`: Night fades from alpha 1 to 0 over 0.4 s (`modulate.a`; a cut under reduced motion) over the round HUD at the round's first second (nothing within reach, so no Aim), then the whole screen is freed.
- The 32 px gaps clear the title plate's expand margins and its base.

## Texts
`pregame.your_role`, `pregame.teammate`, `role.engineer`, `role.dissident`, `role.goal.engineer`, `role.goal.dissident`; in `after`, the HUD's `hud.*`. Every key has en and uk in prime-game-ui `copy/strings.csv`.
