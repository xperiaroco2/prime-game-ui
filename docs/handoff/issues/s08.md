## Goal
Build the map and tasks screen on M in the Toy style, exactly as the UI track drew it, with the how-to card it opens. This is the look of #253's screen and of #254's card; the logic and the look may land in one PR.

## Build
The scene tree, node for node, is in prime-game-ui [`docs/handoff/s08-map.md`](https://github.com/xperiaroco2/prime-game-ui/blob/ui-0.2.0/docs/handoff/s08-map.md) at the tag `ui-0.2.0`. The states are drawn on the review page https://claude.ai/artifact/SCREENS_URL (screen s8; uk and en, default and large text).

- CanvasLayer 3: `Dim` (ToyBackdropDeep), `Tasks` (a raised ToyPanelMenu, top left: the title, one ToySettingRow per task, the time) and `Board` (a raised ToyMapBoard: the rooms, the zones and the pin).
- The how-to card over a second dim (`Dim2`, ToyBackdrop) with Close. The card scene (head, 3 to 4 frames with art and caption, the done frame) is built once here and reused by the loading screen (s3) and the Esc menu's Guide (s5).
- States: `list`, `zone`, `guide`.

Rules:
- Theme type variations from the `ui-0.2.0` pack only (#288). No `add_theme_*_override` and no `theme_override_*` (`theme_test.gd`): gaps come from the spacing variations (`ToyColumn…`, `ToyRow…`), and a gap the scale lacks is the empty `Control` spacer the handoff gives.
- Raised panels and buttons through `ToyRaised` (placement, size and visibility on the wrapper), presses through `ToyPress` (#289).
- Every player-facing text is a deck key through `tr()`. A key with placeholders is set from code with `auto_translate_mode = DISABLED` and rebuilt on `NOTIFICATION_TRANSLATION_CHANGED` (#208). The «?» glyph and the times are data texts with `auto_translate_mode = DISABLED`.
- Sizes and offsets are px at the 1920×1080 base (#287). A size from a variation's size constants is read with `get_theme_constant()` into `custom_minimum_size`, as the node lines give it.
- Icons are the pack's white SVGs, tinted as each node line says, imported at the `svg/scale` the handoff's notes give. The room pictograms are the system B icons (ink, not tinted, no plate), which the `ui-0.2.0` pack does not carry yet.

## Behaviour
- The map key (M, rebindable, #211) opens and closes the screen over the running round: press, not hold. Tab does nothing. The mouse is freed and the player keeps walking; only the mouse look stops (ISSUE_INPUT).
- Tasks grows from its top to its content. One row per task type of the round, in the round's order (content data): the name, a counter only (`map.progress`, live while open), never a description, and a «?» (ToyKeyRoundButton, 42×42, `focus_mode` ALL). Every «?» looks the same. Then the round's time left, m:ss, every second.
- Board: children built from the level's map data (room rects, the pin; #306), scaled to fit. A room shows its rect, its pictogram and its name. The pin is the local player's position, `rotation = PI / 4 + heading` (pivot centre), updated every frame; «Ти тут» follows it without rotating. The handoff's rects are samples until the designer's map exists.
- Zones: hovering a task row (`mouse_entered`, `mouse_exited`) or focusing its «?» lights that task's zones (level or content data) inside their rooms, under the pictogram and name, with the zone tag under the first zone's room (`map.zone_hint`; Switches: `map.zone_hint.switches`). Delivery lights only the storage room, never the rooms still waiting for a package (#255).
- The map never shows players, items, roles, teammates, delivery circles or done points.
- «?» opens that task's how-to card (content data, #254), centred. `Dim2` stops the mouse; while the card is open, `focus_behavior_recursive = FOCUS_BEHAVIOR_DISABLED` on Tasks and Board, restored on close. Close, Esc or M closes only the card (`set_input_as_handled()`). Opened by keyboard or gamepad, Close takes focus and focus returns to that «?» on close; opened with the mouse, closing releases focus.
- Focus: none on open. The first `ui_down` or `ui_up` focuses the first row's «?». Focus moves only with the arrows and the d-pad; the movement keys walk. Space is out of `ui_accept` (ISSUE_INPUT), so a jump never presses a focused «?».
