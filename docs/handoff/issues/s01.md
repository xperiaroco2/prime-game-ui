## Goal
Build the tutorial's UI in the Toy style, exactly as the UI track drew it: the first-launch invite with the language choice, then the current lesson at the top and the lesson list at the right, over the round HUD.

## Build
The scene tree, node for node, is in prime-game-ui [`docs/handoff/s01-tutorial.md`](https://github.com/xperiaroco2/prime-game-ui/blob/ui-0.2.0/docs/handoff/s01-tutorial.md) at the tag `ui-0.2.0`. The states are drawn on the review page https://claude.ai/artifact/SCREENS_URL (screen s1; uk and en, default and large text).

- Invite: `Dim` (ToyBackdrop), `Lang` (two language chips at the top right) and `Box` (ToyPanelDialog, raised) with the title, the body, Start and Skip.
- In the room, on the HUD layer (CanvasLayer 2): `Step`, a 912 px ToyPlate at the top (Progress, Title, and How: a sentence with a keycap, or lesson 1's row of keycaps), and `List`, a 440 px ToyPlate at the right with the nine lessons.
- `Hud` in the handoff is the round HUD without Timer and Role. It is ISSUE_S7's, drawn here only for context.
- States: `invite`, `step`, `step-keys`, `step-howto`.

Rules:
- Theme type variations from the `ui-0.2.0` pack only (#288). No `add_theme_*_override` and no `theme_override_*` (`theme_test.gd`): gaps come from the spacing variations (`ToyColumn…`, `ToyRow…`, `ToyGridList`, `ToyGridSwatch`; `ToyScroll` for a list's gap to its bar), and a gap the scale lacks is the empty `Control` spacer the handoff gives.
- Raised panels and buttons through `ToyRaised` (placement, size and visibility on the wrapper), presses through `ToyPress`, toggles through `ToyToggle` (#289).
- Every player-facing text is a deck key through `tr()` (`tr_n()` for a plural). A key with placeholders, or a sentence drawn in pieces around a keycap, is set from code with `auto_translate_mode = DISABLED` and rebuilt on `NOTIFICATION_TRANSLATION_CHANGED` (#208). Data texts (names, codes, times, numbers, key labels) are set from code with `auto_translate_mode = DISABLED`.
- Sizes and offsets are px at the 1920×1080 base (#287). A size from a variation's size constants is read with `get_theme_constant()` into `custom_minimum_size`, as the node lines give it.
- Icons are the pack's white SVGs, tinted as each node line says, imported at the `svg/scale` the handoff's notes give.

## Behaviour
- The invite shows on the first launch only (a `user://` flag). Focus starts on Start. Start begins lesson 1; Skip, or Esc (`ui_cancel`), goes to the main menu. Both hide the invite for good.
- The language chips are one ButtonGroup. The default is Ukrainian when `OS.get_locale_language()` is `uk`, else English. A press applies at once (`TranslationServer.set_locale`), is saved, and the invite rebuilds its texts (#208). Each language is named in itself.
- Nine lessons, in order. A lesson advances when the game sees its action. The handoff's `Step` note lists each lesson's title key, how key and action: 1 move (a row of keycaps), 2 pick up then put down, 3 hand and belt, 4 deliver, 5 open the map then press «?», 6 downed, 7 death and spectating, 8 voice, 9 the menu. A lesson with two instructions swaps Title and How within one step number.
- How is built in code: `tr()` the how string, split it at `{key}`, `strip_edges()` each piece, one Label per non-empty piece and the keycap between them. The keycap is a ToyKeyOnDark with a ToyKeyText label of the action's bound key (`DisplayServer.keyboard_get_label_from_physical()`, #211). Space and the mouse buttons use `key.space`, `key.mouse_left`, `key.mouse_right`; Space, Shift, Tab and Esc keycaps take the wide size. Lesson 5's «?» is a ToyKeyRound keycap, the look of the map's «?».
- The list is built in code: a done lesson is its name with a check at the right edge, the current one a one-line ToyChipLight, the rest muted and wrapping. The plate widens to the left for the Death lesson's chip.
- The lesson plates take no mouse (`mouse_filter = IGNORE`).
- Lesson 5 opens the map (ISSUE_S8). The Esc menu in the tutorial shows only Game, Guide and Settings (ISSUE_S5). After lesson 9 the game returns to the main menu with no end screen.
- The tutorial room and its stations are level work, outside this issue.
