## Goal
Build the downed, spectating and respawn screens in the Toy style, exactly as the UI track drew them. The life states exist (#145, `life_panel.gd`; spectating #168): this issue reworks their look and moves give-up to the bound key (#211).

## Build
The scene tree, node for node, is in prime-game-ui [`docs/handoff/s09-downed.md`](https://github.com/xperiaroco2/prime-game-ui/blob/ui-0.2.0/docs/handoff/s09-downed.md) at the tag `ui-0.2.0`. The states are drawn on the review page https://claude.ai/artifact/SCREENS_URL (screen s9; uk and en, default and large text).

- `Hud` is the round HUD (ISSUE_S7), the same nodes: this screen hides and returns them.
- It adds, on the HUD layer (CanvasLayer 2): `Downed` (a 688 px ToyPlate at y 152: title, bleed-out bar, time left, or the raise bar), `GiveUp` (a ToyPlate at the bottom centre: the sentence with a keycap and the hold bar), `Spectate` (top centre) and the protection chip.
- States: `down`, `down-holding`, `raise`, `dead`, `back`.

Rules:
- Theme type variations from the `ui-0.2.0` pack only (#288). No `add_theme_*_override` and no `theme_override_*` (`theme_test.gd`): gaps come from the spacing variations (`ToyColumn…`, `ToyRow…`), and a gap the scale lacks is the empty `Control` spacer the handoff gives.
- Every player-facing text is a deck key through `tr()`. A key with placeholders, or the sentence drawn in pieces around the keycap, is set from code with `auto_translate_mode = DISABLED` and rebuilt on `NOTIFICATION_TRANSLATION_CHANGED` (#208). Names, times and the key label are data texts with `auto_translate_mode = DISABLED`.
- Sizes and offsets are px at the 1920×1080 base (#287). A size from a variation's size constants is read with `get_theme_constant()` into `custom_minimum_size`, as the node lines give it.
- Icons are the pack's white SVGs, tinted as each node line says, imported at the `svg/scale` the handoff's notes give.

## Behaviour
- Every node has `mouse_filter = IGNORE`; nothing takes focus. Never show who knocked the player down.
- Down: only Downed, GiveUp and the mic (off) show; the rest of the HUD hides. Downed: `downed.title`, the bleed-out bar (`value` = time left / duration, coloured with the health ramp as the HUD's health) and `downed.time_left` (m:ss, every second).
- GiveUp: `tr("downed.give_up_hold")` split at `{key}`, each piece through `strip_edges()`, an empty piece hidden, with the keycap of the `give_up` binding between them (`DisplayServer.keyboard_get_label_from_physical()`, F by default, #211). The hold bar fills over the 1 s hold while the key is down and empties on release; at 1 the player gives up. It stays visible and empty at rest, so the plate never changes size.
- Raise: while a teammate raises the player, the title becomes `downed.raised_by` with the raiser's name, the raise progress replaces the bleed-out bar and the time, and GiveUp hides. If the teammate stops, the bleed-out bar and the time return.
- Dead: only Spectate shows: `dead.respawn_in` (m:ss) and `dead.watching` with the watched player's name. The camera follows the watched player; `spectate_next` and `spectate_previous` switch. The watched player's HUD, health and role never show; the name updates when they die.
- Back: the round HUD returns, empty hands, stamina full, the mic on again when the player is heard. The protection chip `respawn.protected` counts 3, 2, 1, once a second, 24 px under the timer, then hides.

## Texts
`downed.title`, `downed.time_left`, `downed.raised_by`, `downed.give_up_hold`, `dead.respawn_in`, `dead.watching`, `respawn.protected`, and the HUD's `hud.*` and `role.*`. Every key has en and uk in prime-game-ui `copy/strings.csv`.
