# Round 2, lens E: club-flyer and acid sub-styles (gatherer report, 2026-10-20)

Image URLs are those the fetch tool listed for each page, never fetched; Eye on Design and Nuits Sonores images were
base64 placeholders. Brat's hex comes from the press, not the designer.

## 1. Acid-house and early-rave flyers (1988-93)
Handmade and glossy; no single typeface; photocopy beside fractals; die-cut and folded forms (Shoom's heart, Hedonism's
head); the Plymouth "Acid House Party" A6 flyer in green and black
([The Ransom Note](https://www.theransomnote.com/art-culture/a-brief-history-of-rave-flyers/)). Images: Shoom
https://i.imgur.com/nRkafQI.jpg, Hedonism https://i.imgur.com/ZqhKG8I.jpg, Acid House Party
https://i.imgur.com/O7kGRX1.jpg. Take: corner-cut panels (per-corner radii), green-and-black restraint, a ticket stub
for the lobby. Impossible: fractals, airbrush, gloss.

## 2. The Designers Republic: techno-corporate parody
Eurostile-like wide sans and an in-house unicase face; blocky vector shapes, kana fragments, slogans, brand parody
([Fonts In Use](https://fontsinuse.com/typefaces/246221/amalgamation),
[Creative Bloq](https://www.creativebloq.com/news/wipeout-logo-secret),
[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Designers_Republic), secondary;
[Creative Review on Wipeout](https://www.creativereview.co.uk/an-oral-history-of-wipeout/)). Image:
https://d3e341ktepti6g.cloudfront.net/uploads/2019/10/Wipeout7.jpg. Colour logic (unconfirmed). Take: each device a
fake brand with a slogan and a tiny logotype as flat badges; wide caps; slogan strips as labels. Do not imitate the
Wipeout face ([1997 cease-and-desist account](https://blog.gingerbeardman.com/1997/06/17/cease-and-desist-the-designers-republic/)).

## 3. Berlin techno, systemic (Tresor by Vanja Golubovic and Onlab; Pfadfinderei)
A strict template whose content changes; yearly themes; the strike-through of the other six weekdays
([It's Nice That](https://www.itsnicethat.com/articles/vanja-golubovic-techno-club-posters),
[Eye](https://eyemagazine.com/blog/post/systems-of-nightlife),
[Format](https://www.format.com/magazine/pfadfinderei-design-berlin-electronic-music-moderat)). Images:
https://m.itsnicethat.com/original_images/562f62a57fa44c25920023b3.jpeg?class=w828,
https://live.staticflickr.com/65535/50115524688_b934268f07_b.jpg, Raphael Wicki
https://assets.fontsinuse.com/use-media/74060/upto-700xauto/69b53338/1/jpeg/RaphaelWicki_Poster.jpeg
([page](https://fontsinuse.com/uses/23066/techno-andamp-house-nacht)). Take: grid, mono numerals, big type, a hairline
rule, a struck-through strip for state; acid green as the one rare colour. The most Godot-native.

## 4. Acid graphics 2018-25
Warped, stretched, cropped type; 3D backgrounds; op-art; much neon green
([Eye on Design](https://eyeondesign.aiga.org/acid-graphics-are-the-new-psychedelia-with-a-heady-dose-of-cynicism/);
[Liquid Sky 2019](https://fontsinuse.com/uses/51895/liquid-sky-2019-posters): Nickel stretched, all caps, outlined,
cropped, asterisks). Images:
https://assets.fontsinuse.com/static/use-media-items/184/183687/upto-700xauto/69b59b87/lsky3.webp,
https://assets.fontsinuse.com/static/use-media-items/184/183685/upto-700xauto/69b59b87/lsky1.webp. Possible: width-axis
caps, words clipped by their panel, asterisk dingbats, flat stripes as rectangles. Impossible: warped type, 3D, op-art
textures.

## 5. Y2K chrome and hyperpop
Chrome, inflated forms, pixel-cut shapes; one loud element on a calm layout
([Made Good Designs](https://madegooddesigns.com/y2k-graphic-design/), a blog). Chrome and gradients impossible; a
flat two-tone silver can hint. Weakest fit for the adult tone.

## 6. One-green monochrome: Brat and Nuits Sonores
Brat (Brent David Freaney, Special Offer, Inc.): lowercase Arial, slightly stretched, on a garish green chosen from
about 500, reportedly #8ACE00 ([Fonts In Use](https://www.fontsinuse.com/uses/61357/charli-xcx-brat-album-art-and-campaign),
[DJ Mag](https://djmag.com/news/charli-xcx-brat-artwork-designer-shares-creative-process),
[The Nightly](https://thenightly.com.au/politics/brat-green-aka-hex-8ace00-you-cant-escape-this-colour-thanks-to-charlie-xcx-c-15494520)).
Nuits Sonores 2025 (DIA Studio): black and white, neon green added on site; the team avoided saturated colour,
trance-era type, glitch and nostalgia ([Design Compass](https://designcompass.org/en/2025/12/18/nuits-sonores-smoky-music-festival-branding/),
[It's Nice That](https://www.itsnicethat.com/articles/dia-studio-nuits-sonores-graphic-design-project-181125); image
https://m.itsnicethat.com/original_images/NS_Poster_05.jpg?class=w1080). Take: flat lime panels with black lowercase
text; green, black, white only. Keep lime cards from sitting behind lime-headed aliens.

## 7. Japanese neon and city graphics
Dense signage, kana as texture, vaporwave
([Yokogao](https://www.yokogaomag.com/editorial/japanese-vaporwave-art-culture-of-the-1990s),
[Airline Industries](https://urbmagazine.substack.com/p/interview-with-airline-industries)). Accent only; decorative
Japanese on a Ukrainian and English UI is a cultural-care risk.

## 8. UK garage flyers
Every font and colour, turquoise and ocean motifs, PVC
([It's Nice That, Ewen Spencer](https://www.itsnicethat.com/articles/ewen-spencer)). Take: mixed type in one panel, a
turquoise next to lime. Busy if overdone.

## 9. Queer club nights
Homodrop by Benedikt Rugar: lurid colour, absurd still lifes ([Dalston Superstore](https://dalstonsuperstore.com/benedikt-rugar/);
https://dalstonsuperstore.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/homodrop--724x1024.jpg); Gina Guasch Studio's illustrated
characters ([Creative Boom](https://www.creativeboom.com/work/gina-guasch-studios-designs-go-beyond-trends-and-are-full-of-colour-and-life/),
snippet only); Horse Meat Disco's DIY neon horse ([Movement](https://movementfestival.com/pages/horse-meat-disco)).
Take: absurd objects as flat sticker icons, the logo as a deadpan joke; fits crude adult humour best.

## Gatherer's ranking of five variants
1. tDR techno-corporate parody: lime on near-black, an alarm orange for "busted".
2. Berlin systemic grid: black, white, grey, lime only on the active thing.
3. One-green monochrome (Brat, Nuits Sonores): flat lime cards, plain lowercase type.
4. Acid graphics stretched and cropped: lime with violet or ultramarine.
5. Queer-club sticker maximalism with a UKG turquoise: lime, hot pink, turquoise.
Reserve: the rave die-cut corner as a shape language for any variant; Japanese neon as an accent only.
