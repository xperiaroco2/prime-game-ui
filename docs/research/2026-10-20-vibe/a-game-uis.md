## Vibe families
1. **Neon club flyer** (Persona 5, Hotline Miami 2, Neon White, Bomb Rush Cyberfunk, Splatoon): hard tilted colour
   blocks, huge caps, a loud selection. Risk: busy over a dark 3D scene. Skew is not a StyleBoxFlat: node rotation or
   none.
2. **Late-night TV show graphics** (Fall Guys, Content Warning, Splatoon, Hi-Fi Rush): lower thirds, LIVE bugs,
   scoreboards, tickers. Fits "aliens redo Earth formats". Risk: 2000s corporate with plain type. Very Godot-safe.
3. **Alien-misread Earth tech** (Lethal Company, R.E.P.O., Atomic Heart, Pacific Drive, Schedule I): boxy devices
   with one voice. Risk: sterile industrial. A voice for in-world devices, not the whole UI.
4. **Glossy designer-toy packaging** (High on Life, Fall Guys, Party Animals): highest childish risk; gloss is a
   gradient.
5. **Comic sticker and street tag** (Bomb Rush Cyberfunk, Hi-Fi Rush, Splatoon): suits how-to cards and "busted"
   posters; clashes with outline-free PBR characters; hatching only inside card art.

Gatherer's quick take: 1 and 2 together fit best; 3 for in-world devices.
