## A. Acid flyer
90s techno flyer meets 2020s acid graphics: fluorescent on near-black, unruly but readable type.
Images: [Maiden Voyage 2024, Fonts In Use](https://fontsinuse.com/uses/63300/maiden-voyage-festival-2024):
`https://assets.fontsinuse.com/static/use-media-items/236/235679/upto-700xauto/69b5e6c1/Maiden%20Voyage%20Festival.png`,
`https://assets.fontsinuse.com/static/use-media-items/236/235681/upto-700xauto/69b5e6c1/MV%202024.png`,
`https://assets.fontsinuse.com/static/use-media-items/236/235680/upto-700xauto/69b5e6c1/MV%20tickets.png`;
[daisyUI acid graphics](https://trends.daisyui.com/trend/acid-graphics/): `29-inspiration-nightclub-laser.avif`,
`09-acid-graphics.avif`, `02-colors-and-graphics-chaoqun-xu.avif`.
Facts: Tempel Softland Condensed with Space Grotesk, rounded outlined fields, amorphous shapes; acid = fluorescent
palette, warped type, one sober info panel ([daisyUI](https://trends.daisyui.com/trend/acid-graphics/),
[Eye on Design](https://eyeondesign.aiga.org/acid-graphics-are-the-new-psychedelia-with-a-heady-dose-of-cynicism/)).
Rules: 8-16 px radii, pill buttons, 3 px borders, wild condensed caps for titles only, calm grotesk or mono for text.
Palette: bg #120B1F, pd #1E1433, pl #F2F0E6, text #F4F1FF, acid #C8F000, orange #FF6A1A, danger #FF5A6A, success
#3DDC97, muted #9A8FB8. Text on pd 15.69, acid on pd 13.23, ink on acid 14.56. Orange melts into coral heads (1.11).

## C. Neubrutal press kit
Flat blocks, heavy ink outlines, hard offset shadows; the nearest relative of the engineer's comic card reference.
Images: [neubrutalism](https://trends.daisyui.com/trend/neubrutalism/) `04-neubrutalism`, `08-neubrutalism`,
`23-inspiration-black-outline-sign`; [halftone comic](https://trends.daisyui.com/trend/halftone-comic-book/)

shadow collapses.

#FF5A47, success #1FBF6B, muted #A89F95 / #5C554E. Text on pd 15.45, ink on yellow 13.32.

#5FD38D, muted #8C8C96. Least likely to fight the characters.

## Neon under the Godot-safe limits
1. Tube: pd fill, a 2-3 px border in the tube colour, one shadow offset 0 size 8-12 in the tube colour at 40-50 %;
   near-white text with a 2-3 px darker coloured outline.
2. Hard slab: fluorescent fill, 3 px ink border, one hard shadow (4,4) size 0; hover (6,6), press (0,0).
3. Lit and unlit: off is pd, muted border and text; on is accent fill, ink text and the tube halo.

## Gatherer's lean (judgment)
C's structure with F's discipline and D's neutral dominance; A's flyer type for titles only.
