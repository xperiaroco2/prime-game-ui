2. **Kidrobot and Pop Mart**: the shop is a neutral stage (Kidrobot theme-color #F5F5F5), numbered "01-06" lists,
   NEW and Preorder badges, all-caps heads ([kidrobot.com](https://www.kidrobot.com)); Pop Mart's all-caps buttons and

5. **Hirono (Pop Mart)**: pensive, puffy-eyed, expressionless, muted
   ([JoongAng](https://www.koreajoongangdaily.com/business/popular-character-hirono-finds-a-permanent-home-with-a-themed-store-in-gwangjang-market/12510657)).

7. **Space age**: tin robots in lithographed boxes
   ([Science Museum](https://collection.sciencemuseumgroup.org.uk/objects/co8538872/atom-robot)); a 1960s blister card

9. **Adult toys**: premium finishes and sophisticated palettes

## Toy-like but adult (synthesis)
One silhouette, many skins; matte fills, at most a flat catch-light dot; flat affect; numbers and editions; neutral
chrome with the character as the colour; condensed caps for labels, wide caps for titles, a quiet sans for text;
space-age shapes as flat polygons; a reveal as a withheld card; humour in copy and naming; a pressed-vinyl hard
shadow in a darker shade of the fill.

## Childish traps
Rainbow headers; bubble or comic-balloon fonts and wobbly baselines; scattered stars and confetti; maximum radius
everywhere; pastel candy with white text; mascot faces on buttons, exclamation marks; gloss, gradients and glow; the
cream-panel Toy look already rejected.
