### What the sources say about the flow (text, no images)
- **Tablet** (official site): an in-game tablet holds the map, how-to for every task and info on most things.
  UPDATE 0.12.0 moved many tablet features to a new menu (the Esc menu). UPDATE 1.0.0 redesigned the tablet with
  shortcuts to menus and tutorials, and added an indicator when a player is in the pause menu.
- **Stamina and health** (official site): stamina absorbs part of the damage; with no stamina, actions cost health.
- **Voice** (official site, Vice): proximity voice; players can isolate themselves or hear a far-off call for help.
- **HUD**: UPDATE 0.14.2 added a key (U) to hide or show the HUD (so marketing shots may have no HUD).
- **Lobby**: a walkable 3D room (wall panels guide new players, 0.14.0); named lobbies; host session settings.
- **Roles**: Employees and Dissidents; dissidents recognise each other. A Steam thread suggests the "red ball" over
  dissidents' heads stay visible back in the lobby after a match, so at that time the post-game lobby did not say who
  was who.
- **Death**: the dead become ghosts who follow the living (Vice); UPDATE 0.14.2 lets ghosts blink (teleport). A Steam
  thread asks for a real spectator mode.
- **No meetings or votes** (NamuWiki, via search summary).
