# Card art for the how-to cards: research

Research for [prime-game-ui#3](https://github.com/xperiaroco2/prime-game-ui/issues/3) (the drawing style of the how-to cards), 2026-10-07. Three read-only lenses: what makes wordless card art readable and funny, a plasticine look that Godot 4.7.2 draws the same as a browser, and the facts the cards must show. Web pages were read as HTML, JSON or text only; nothing was downloaded. "(unconfirmed)" marks a secondary source, a search snippet or a claim with no source. The brief the illustrators draw from is [`pages/card-art/brief.md`](../../../pages/card-art/brief.md).

The engineer's direction for this wave: a fun cartoon drawing; the game's characters look a bit like plasticine; the UI style is Toy (chunky rounded shapes, thick plum outline, cream panels, sunny yellow). Every option is a fun cartoon.

## 1. What makes 3-4 wordless panels readable and funny

- **Pictures over words, bright and solid.** Overcooked moved its UI from text to pictures so players grasp it at a glance, and kept it bright and solid so it reads from a distance ([Cane and Rinse, Ghost Town Games](https://caneandrinse.com/ghost-town-games/)). It teaches a recipe as visual shorthand ([Superjump](https://www.superjumpmagazine.com/overcooked-how-design-creates-teamwork/), secondary) and leans on what players already know, which matters when four learn at once ([Co-Optimus interview](https://www.co-optimus.com/ampfeature/1838/indie-ana-co-op-and-the-overcooked-interview.html)).
- **Low detail stays readable with flat shading and an outline** ([PlayStation Blog, A Short Hike](https://blog.playstation.com/2021/08/05/crafting-a-tiny-open-world-a-look-behind-the-scenes-at-the-creation-of-a-short-hike/)).
- **Design the figure around the action the card shows.** Moving Out locked "hands clearly visible" early because play depends on them ([PlayStation Blog](https://blog.playstation.com/archive/2020/02/04/death-squared-creator-returns-with-multiplayer-puzzler-moving-out-launching-28th-april-on-ps4)). Here: carrying with both hands, pulling a lever.
- **One blank, consistent character.** Human: Fall Flat's hero is a featureless white figure ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human:_Fall_Flat)); simple iconic characters ease identification and the reader fills the gap between panels, so each panel needs one clear change ([Wikipedia, Understanding Comics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Understanding_Comics)). Among Us tells players apart by colour and silhouette ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Among_Us); nothing on its task art, unconfirmed).
- **One step per picture, uncluttered.** IKEA's wordless manuals open with the finished product, show one step per page, split overloaded pages after a 1999 study and test that a person assembles with no text ([Justin Zhuang](https://justinzhuang.com/posts/wordless-instructions/), secondary). Step-by-step action diagrams ([Agrawala et al. 2003](https://graphics.stanford.edu/papers/assembly_instructions/), abstract only).
- **Direction by arrows and motion lines.** Arrows read as function or sequence ([Heiser and Tversky 2006](https://www.tc.columbia.edu/faculty/bt2158/faculty-profile/files/sincomprehendingandproducingmechanicaldiagrams.PDF), search summary, unconfirmed). Motion lines run behind a moving object along its path ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motion_lines)). Manga cues stand for many emotions ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manga_iconography)); avoiding them for a mixed audience is my inference.
- **Pictograms are misread more than designers think.** Briefing-card pictorials scored 28.8 to 96.3 % comprehension, mean 65 %; only 8.3 % passed the ANSI 85 % bar ([FAA, Corbett et al. 2008](https://rosap.ntl.bts.gov/view/dot/58470)). So: redundant cues (lever and lamp, sign and arrow) and a test on someone new to the game. Safety cards are illustrated to overcome language limits ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aircraft_safety_card); its "retained longer" claim is unreferenced, unconfirmed).
- **One gag, and the gag must not hide the step.** A standard briefing gave 47 % more recall of key messages than a humorous one ([Atlas of Science](https://atlasofscience.org/does-humour-undervalue-the-importance-of-safety-information-contained-in-pre-flight-safety-briefings/)). Wordless one-panel gags are a known form ([Wikipedia, gag cartoon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gag_cartoon)). Staging: one idea, unmistakably clear; anticipation and exaggerated squash and stretch for comedy ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twelve_basic_principles_of_animation)).
- **Thin sources.** No text source on the card art of PEAK or LOCKDOWN Protocol ([Steam appdetails](https://store.steampowered.com/api/appdetails?appids=2780980)); unconfirmed.

Synthesis (mine): one action per panel, the same figure in every panel, big shapes, thick outline, flat colour; before and after shown by state; one gag per card placed on the simplest step and acting the step out; no text, numbers or manga cues; captions carry the words.

## 2. The facts the cards must show

- **The characters are plasticine people** (the engineer, 2026-10-06), not the superseded faceted low-poly look of the art repo's `docs/pipeline.md`, `docs/faces.md` and lens-1 ([prime-game-art#16](https://github.com/xperiaroco2/prime-game-art/issues/16), [#34](https://github.com/xperiaroco2/prime-game-art/issues/34)). Seen in local renders under `D:/prime-art-raw/review/facelab/` (viewed, not copied; the private wave-A page was not opened).
- **Body:** an ordinary adult of about 1.75 m, slim visible neck, an egg head scaled x1.3 (about 17 x 24 cm, about 7 heads tall by my calculation), A-pose arms, five-finger hands, small flat shoes, chunky soft clothes; lanky bodies were rejected ([prime-game-art#14](https://github.com/xperiaroco2/prime-game-art/issues/14)).
- **Face:** big glossy round white eyes with a tall dark pill pupil and a thin lid; thick slab brows; a big ball nose a little lighter and pinker than the skin; a small low gap-tooth grin. Matte finger-pressed clay, no outline, soft warm light; hair in flat leaf-like locks (local renders and `people_report.json`).
- **Colours** (sampled from lit renders, approximate, unconfirmed): light skin about #c9b3a5, dark about #7b5940; muted earthy clothes with a few pops (teal about #78ada5). No per-player colour ([ui-decisions](../../ui-decisions.md), Style).
- **Movement:** baked clips only, no ragdoll; a package takes both hands ([prime-game-art#16](https://github.com/xperiaroco2/prime-game-art/issues/16); `D:/prime-game/content/items/package.tres`). How a fall looks: unconfirmed.
- **Delivery:** packages start in the storage room, have no colours and carry their destination's plain pictogram like a stamp; the same sign marks the room on the map and on a plate by its door; the spot is a lit zone, one per room; no destination marker ([prime-game#255](https://github.com/xperiaroco2/prime-game/issues/255); [ui-decisions](../../ui-decisions.md), Delivery). The game code still has the old colour circles; the card shows the new rule.
- **Room signs:** system B pictograms (48 grid) and plates (64 grid), own work ([LICENCES.json](../../../pages/room-signs/systems/b/LICENCES.json)).
- **Switches:** only the copy deck defines it ([copy/strings.csv](../../../copy/strings.csv)). Its look and whether "done" means all lit at once or for some seconds: unconfirmed. The card draws a generic lever switch with a lamp.
- **Where the art shows:** keep-aspect-centred, 120 px high in frames about 336 px wide on the map card and the Esc menu ([s08](../../handoff/s08-map.md), [s05](../../handoff/s05-esc-menu.md)), 200 px high on the loading card ([s03](../../handoff/s03-connecting.md)). A 4:3 panel shows at 160 x 120 and 267 x 200. Frames are white with a 3 px plum outline; the finish frame is mint-tint #d5f4ec ([howto tokens](../../../tokens/components/howto.tokens.json)).
- **Captions:** the copy deck keys `howto.delivery.*` and `howto.switches.*`; the art carries no text.

## 3. What Godot 4.7.2 draws (the ground of the SVG contract)

- Godot 4.7.2 bundles ThorVG 1.0.3 with SVG, raw and PNG loaders and no TTF loader ([thirdparty/README.md](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/4.7.2-stable/thirdparty/README.md), [config.h](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/4.7.2-stable/thirdparty/thorvg/inc/config.h)); import and `Image.load_svg_from_buffer` share one path ([image_loader_svg.cpp](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/4.7.2-stable/modules/svg/image_loader_svg.cpp)).
- Parsed elements: [tvgSvgLoader.cpp](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/4.7.2-stable/thirdparty/thorvg/src/loaders/svg/tvgSvgLoader.cpp#L2132-L2170); `pattern`, `marker`, `switch`, `foreignObject` and every filter primitive but `feGaussianBlur` are ignored. ThorVG's README advertises more than Godot draws ([thorvg](https://github.com/thorvg/thorvg)).
- Tested here (Godot 4.7.2 headless against headless Chrome and Edge at 2x; scratch harness, not in the repo): paths, shapes, strokes, gradients, opacity, evenodd, paint-order, clipPath, use, transforms, XML header and comments match (mean diff at most about 0.9 of 255); contract-style cards 0.55 to 0.78; a test panel of the brief 0.07.
- Fails or ignored (tested): `text`; gradients inside `mask`; `pattern`; nested `svg`; `feDropShadow`, anisotropic blur; `rgba()`, `hsla()`, `#rrggbbaa` (drawn black); `visibility`, `mix-blend-mode`, `vector-effect`; `<style>` beyond `.class`.
- Cost: 40 panels at 2x about 47 MiB RGBA8, 1.6 to 2.7 MB on disk, 3.7 ms per card at import; a 0.5 s stall every 18 to 32 runtime rasterisations (cause unconfirmed), so import, never rasterise at runtime ([importing images](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/tutorials/assets_pipeline/importing_images.html), [DPITexture](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/classes/class_dpitexture.html) experimental).
- **Plasticine in flat vector:** inflated forms lit from the top left, a shade shape, white highlight blobs, a cast shadow, at most one soft gradient per body ([Setproduct](https://preview.setproduct.com/blog/claymorphism-design-guide), [UX Misfit](https://uxmisfit.com/?p=3465), [LogRocket](https://blog.logrocket.com/ux-design/what-is-claymorphism-web-design/)); highlight and shade as separate shapes ([Envato Tuts+](https://design.tutsplus.com/tutorials/quick-tip-how-to-cell-shade-and-add-texture-to-a-vector-comic-character--vector-5053)), colours baked (no blend modes). Aardman keeps fingerprints on purpose ([Science and Media Museum](https://blog.scienceandmediamuseum.org.uk/surprising-stories-about-aardman-animations/), [KCL](https://www.kcl.ac.uk/news/comment-in-the-age-of-ai-wallace-and-gromits-claymation-style-remains-a-festive-favourite)); faint vector arcs for them is my inference (unconfirmed).

## 4. Open for the engineer (taste)

1. **Outline or none:** the Toy UI has a thick plum outline, the characters none; the four styles cover both.
2. **Card figure proportions:** a 7-heads figure has a ~7 px face at 160 x 120, so the brief draws 3 to 4.5 heads (per style).
3. **The 4:3 box:** the map card's art area is about 336 x 120, so a 4:3 panel leaves space beside it; kept as set for this wave.
4. **The switch's look** is invented for the card until the game defines it.

## 5. Art-source options (who draws, licences, costs)

| Option | Licence fit | Cost | Fit |
|---|---|---|---|
| **(a) Agent-drawn SVG** (recommended) | Own work; outputs assigned to the customer ([Anthropic commercial terms](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/commercial-terms)); AI output is protected only where a human determined expressive elements ([copyright.gov](https://copyright.gov/newsnet/2025/1060.html); my reading, not legal advice). | Agent tokens, about 0.25 to 0.45M per agent (project calibration). | Exact palette, outline and contract; fast gag iteration. |
| **(b) Commissioned illustrator** | Own work by written assignment. | UK median about GBP 350 a day, full copyright up to 3x ([Creative Boom 2026](https://www.creativeboom.com/insight/illustrator-pricing-in-2026/)); spots USD 200 to 400 ([Creative Boom guide](https://www.creativeboom.com/tips/freelance-illustration-rates/), [Format](https://www.format.com/magazine/resources/illustration/who-pays-illustration-jobs); unconfirmed); two cards roughly USD 1,500 to 4,000 (my estimate with [RocketBrush](https://rocketbrush.com/blog/game-art-outsourcing-prices-complete-pricing-breakdown); unconfirmed). | A human hand; a money decision. |
| **(c) CC0 packs** | Kenney CC0 ([kenney.nl](https://kenney.nl/support), [OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org/content/toon-characters-1)). | Free; download needs a yes. | Generic, not Toy; fit unconfirmed. Reference at most. |
| **(d) Emoji sets** | Fluent Emoji MIT ([GitHub API](https://api.github.com/repos/microsoft/fluentui-emoji)), Noto Apache-2.0; Twemoji CC-BY and OpenMoji CC-BY-SA not allowed. | Free; download needs a yes. | Single icons, no scenes. |
| **(e) AI image generators** | Not recommended: no non-infringement warranty ([Midjourney terms](https://docs.midjourney.com/hc/en-us/articles/32083055291277-Terms-of-Service)); Firefly allows commercial use ([Adobe FAQ](https://helpx.adobe.com/firefly/web/get-started/learn-the-basics/adobe-firefly-faq.html)); OpenAI terms page 403 ([terms.law](https://terms.law/ai-output-rights/chatgpt/), unconfirmed). | Subscription. | Raster, fails the SVG contract; weak at a consistent figure (assessment). |

Recommendation: (a), four agent-drawn style directions from the brief, the engineer picks one; (b) only if the engineer wants a human hand (a money decision).
