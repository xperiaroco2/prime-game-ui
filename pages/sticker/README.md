# The ink and mint screens page

`index.html` shows the engineer's pick of round 3 of the vibe research, direction 4 "Ink and mint sticker"
(`docs/research/2026-10-20-vibe/`), on five screens (main menu, lobby, round HUD, Esc settings, round end), with a
switch between three strengths of the style. Published as the private artifact
https://claude.ai/artifact/HtfG17WM8Wok5TqMkrQZkf. Hand-written, no build step; fonts load from Google Fonts; the
chosen strength is remembered in the viewer's browser only.

`img/` is not committed (`.gitignore`): `img/estate.jpg` and `img/alien.jpg` are the same copies of the art track's
concept art as on the vibe page (`pages/vibe/README.md`). To republish, recreate them and publish `index.html` with
`files` mapping both.
